Initial Brainstorming Ideas

Music Player: plays local and streamed music, with playlists, a queue, and a “now playing” screen with lock screen controls.
Clothing Shopping App: browse clothing by category, size, and price, save favorites, and check out from a cart.
Car Shopping App: browse new and used car listings, filter by make, model, year, price, and mileage, and compare cars side by side.
Dating App: create a profile with photos and interests, swipe on matches, and chat once both people like each other.
Concert Tickets App: discover upcoming concerts near you by artist, genre, or date, save events, and see ticket prices and venue details.
Workout Tracker: log exercises, sets, and reps, track progress over time with charts, and save workout routines.
Recipe & Meal Planner: search recipes by ingredients you have, plan meals for the week, and generate a grocery list automatically.
Campus Study Group Finder: students post or join study sessions by class, time, and location on campus.

Evaluation of Top 3 Ideas

Concert Tickets App: This one feels really made for a phone. It can use your location to find shows nearby, send notifications when tickets drop, and pull up the venue on Maps. The idea is simple: one spot to find concerts for artists you actually like, without jumping between a bunch of sites. Pretty much anyone who goes to shows could use it, and they’d keep coming back to check new announcements. Scope-wise it’s doable since the Ticketmaster API gives real event data for free. Actually buying tickets in the app could be a stretch goal.

Music Player: People would use this every day, and it gets to do cool phone stuff like background playback and lock screen controls. The problem is competing with Spotify and Apple Music, since it’s hard to give someone a reason to switch. Licensing is also a pain, so it’d mostly be stuck with local files or short song previews.

Dating App: This would definitely keep people coming back, and it uses the camera, location, and notifications well. But it’s a lot. Matching, live chat, photo storage, and safety features are tough to pull off in our timeline. On top of that, it only works if a lot of people are already on it.

Final App Idea Chosen

We’re going with the Concert Tickets App! It hits the sweet spot. It’s useful, it uses real phone features, it gives people a reason to come back, and it’s something we can actually finish and make look good. The other two were fun ideas, but they were either too crowded or too big for our timeline.
